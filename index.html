Where it began

Inspired by family.
Made for every busy mind.

Wiggleworm began with a wish to help children feel understood. There can be so much happening inside a child that nobody else sees: the meaning behind a big plan, the effort inside a reaction, or a lonely moment tucked between the laughter.

Wiggleworm gives that inner world a place on the page. Wiggleworm makes mistakes, asks for help, gathers friends and tries again. The grown-ups learn, too.
