Privacy

Reader updates

The signup form asks for an adult’s email address and consent to receive Hello Wiggleworm updates. We use EmailOctopus to manage the list, send confirmation messages and deliver email. You must confirm your email address before joining the subscribed list. Signup confirmation and subscription preferences are handled by EmailOctopus. You receive a confirmation request when you sign up.

The embedded form and its service providers may process technical information such as IP address and browser information when the form loads or is used. The form uses Google reCAPTCHA for bot protection. See EmailOctopus’s privacy policy and Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for their processing details.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe using the link in an update email. To ask about your information, request removal or get help, contact Victor Corrales at hello@hellowiggleworm.com. An unsubscribe record may be retained to help prevent future mailings to that address.

Children’s information

Our updates are for grown-up readers. We do not ask for a child’s name, diagnosis or personal story. Please do not submit children’s personal information through the form or by email.

Website and purchases

No checkout, analytics or advertising pixels are installed on this website. The hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the website.

This page describes the current implementation. We will update it when services or data practices change, including before purchases open.
